Approach

What I bring that isn't in the stack list.

01

Validate before modelling

Teaching chemistry and running lab assays means I do not skip the hypothesis. I question the data first, learn the domain second, and build the model third. Most errors live upstream of the algorithm.

02

Explain, or it does not get used

A model stakeholders do not trust sits unused. SHAP, feature importance, and a plain-language summary ship alongside every metric. On Candor that principle became the product: every score carries a written reason, and a human always makes the call.

03

Build for the day it breaks

Working on a system holding protected health information changes how you build. Signed sessions, least-privilege access, an audit trail, and a migration plan that survives cutover are not extras. They are the job.
